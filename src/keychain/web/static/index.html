Choose a picture to start.

Picture

A project keeps the picture with the masks, lines, colours and settings.

Colours

One per filament. The darkest is the ink: the lines. A dark shade prints one thin layer of its colour over ink; a light shade prints one thin layer of the lightest colour over its colour.

    Paint

    Paint a colour where the picture should have it, such as a faint strip of hair or an area to print as a shade.

    Brush
    Paints the colour where you drag.
    Spline
    Click around an area and click the first point again to fill it with the colour, for small detailed areas. Or click the last point again to replace a harsh edge with a smooth one. Click on another curve to start or end exactly on it; hold Alt to place a point freely. Escape cancels. Move over a spline to see its points, and drag one to move it.
    Erase
    Takes off paint and splines.
    What to keep rough is fine

    Where the picture's background is guessed wrong. Choose what to mark, then how.

    Background
    Leave this part out. The colours are then found again in what is left; the ones you added or changed stay.
    Keep
    Part of the keychain, even if it looks like background.
    Erase
    Takes off background and keep.
    Cut
    The keychain is exactly the shape drawn: all round it is left out, all of it is kept, paint too. Draw it with Spline, Circle or Rectangle.
    Brush
    Paint it on.
    Spline
    Click points round an area, and the first point again to close it.
    Circle
    Press at the centre and drag out, or click the centre and then the rim.
    Rectangle
    Drag from corner to corner, or click both corners.
    Fillet
    Click rectangle corners to round them by the radius in the field, or a rectangle's outline for all four. A radius typed rounds all the corners chosen at once.
    Dimension
    Press D, then click a circle for its diameter or a rectangle's edge for its width or height, and type the size in mm. It stays on the picture and keeps that size while the handles are dragged; click its value to change it, Delete on it takes it off.
    Shapes
    Drag a handle to change one. Click its outline to type its sizes in mm. Delete takes off the shape under the pointer.
    Details
    Smooth
    Calmer curves there.
    Detailed
    Follow the picture more closely there.
    Connect
    Click A, then B, to join an area that fades out in between, such as a strip of hair.
    Erase
    Takes off smooth, detailed and connect lines.
    Text on the back

    From behind, the picture shows mirrored, so text reads backwards. Click points round the text, and it reads the right way round on the back as well. The preview then shows the back too: drag the dashed area there to move the text, such as onto the middle of a speech bubble, and turn it or make it smaller in the fields under it where the back has less room for it.

    Click the first point again to close the area; click inside an area to take it off. Escape cancels.

    Size and smoothing

    Without the rim and the ring. Tick one and the other follows from the picture; tick both and the picture fits within them. The preview shows both below and beside it.

    How wide the tab with the hole meets the keychain: a larger neck is a wider, sturdier curve into it.

    The rim, the ring and the core between the two faces. A light rim keeps the picture's outer lines visible, such as strands of hair. Click the swatch for any other colour, such as green: a filament of its own.

    How far the spline curves may stray from the picture: higher is smoother, with fewer points.
